# Your slice: check the claims in part of the note

Read `verify/brief.md` first, then the note's header (lines 1–25), then your
slice. Your slice's name and line ranges are in the message that started you.
Your scratch directory is `verify/<slice-name>/`.

Check every claim in your line ranges, as the brief defines a claim, against
its evidence. That includes each provenance line and the numbers above it
against the result file it names. Outside your ranges, read only as much as
you need to understand a claim in them.
